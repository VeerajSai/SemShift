# Code of Conduct

SemShift follows the Contributor Covenant.

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect maintainer time and project scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Bad-faith security reports or exploit demonstrations in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

Report concerns privately through GitHub Security Advisories when they involve security-sensitive material.

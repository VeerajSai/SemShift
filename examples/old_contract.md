# Service Agreement

## Disputes

Any dispute arising under this agreement may be brought in the courts of the customer's
home jurisdiction. Either party may pursue claims individually or as part of a class action.

## Liability

The provider is responsible for direct damages caused by its negligence, up to the fees
paid in the prior twelve months.

## Termination

Either party may terminate with thirty days' written notice. Prepaid fees are refunded
on a pro-rata basis.

# Draft Abstract

We evaluate LensRank on the MMLU benchmark and compare against a BM25 baseline.
The method improves accuracy from 71.2% to 73.4% on our validation split.

## Limitations

The study is preliminary. We do not evaluate multilingual datasets, and results may not generalize to long-context tasks.

## Conclusion

LensRank suggests that lightweight reranking can improve retrieval quality in narrow documentation domains.
